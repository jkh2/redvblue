    RedvBlue

    The 2024 county map, cut into 246 regions: neighboring counties in a state that voted the same way form one region. Both sides play by the same rules. You choose what the land pays.

    How the map was made

    Every county takes the color of the party that won it in the 2024 presidential race. Within each state, counties that touch and voted the same way are merged into one region. That gives 246 regions: 66 red and 180 blue. Red regions are few and large; blue regions are many and mostly small.

    Rules

    • Every region starts with 3 armies.
    • Each turn a side gets 3 armies, plus its share of 30 armies by the income measure you chose, plus 1 for every whole state it holds.
    • Attack a neighboring region by dragging onto it. Battles use Risk-style dice: up to 3 attacking dice against up to 2 defending, highest dice compared, ties to the defender. A drag keeps rolling until one side breaks; the winners march in, leaving one behind.
    • Once per turn, move armies between any two of your regions that connect through your own territory. Moving ends your turn.
    • A side with fewer than 25 regions (10% of the map) collapses and loses.
    • Dashed lines are sea links: Alaska to western Washington, Hawaii to Los Angeles, and islands to their nearest neighbor.
